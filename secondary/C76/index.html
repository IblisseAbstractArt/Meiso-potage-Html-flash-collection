Comic Market 76 Participation Information

8/15 (Sat) A-70b

We will be distributing our new Touhou fan-made Flash animation collection CD, "Touhou Gouryuuden".

In addition, we plan to bring in some older CDs by Meisou Potage. Thank you for your support.

Apology regarding some display issues

Currently, regarding the new title "Touhou Gouryuuden," the following display issues have been discovered in the "Bunka-chou Mode" photography game and in the included game "Rezamari E-MISSION."

[Touhou Gouryuuden Bunkachou Mode]

When taking a photo, the silver (gold) score displayed on top of the photo may differ from the actual score.
(The actual score is calculated based on the score displayed in the details on the left.)

[Rezamari E-MISSION]

In some stages, enemy bullets are displayed with different images than usual.
(Hit detection and power remain unchanged.)

Fortunately, these display glitches will not hinder gameplay, but please be aware that you may experience some discomfort while playing. We sincerely apologize for not being able to address this issue before the product release.

Regarding future responses to these bugs, we are currently considering within our group whether we can distribute correction patches online. We will announce our response plan on this site as soon as it is decided, so we would appreciate it if you would check back occasionally.
