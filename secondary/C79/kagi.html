■■■　Flash Screensaver　Miracle∞Hinacle　■■■

Hello there. This is Kagi from Meisou Potage.

Um, regarding the collection for Comiket 79, I had originally planned to make
a proper, substantial packaged product, but I finally had to cancel it. I'm sorry.
So, I made a screensaver for a work I created in the past called Miracle∞Hinacle.

Um, I imagine that using this will require fairly decent specs.
In which case, you might say I shouldn't have made something like this in the first place. Sorry.
I actually wanted to include a clock and such, but it became absurdly heavy, so that wasn't possible.
I wonder how the people at tha make screensavers...

And this is Windows-only?! I apologize to Mac users.
I'm an anti-Apple person, after all. Breaking an iPad today in gratitude.
Well, actually, the screensaver-generation app simply wasn't compatible with Mac.
I've included just the swf file for now, so if you'd like to see what it's like,
please open it in your browser to check it out...

Anyway, I'm sorry it turned out so embarrassingly poor.
(Even though I tried all sorts of things through trial and error...)
I think the next one will be for Reitaisai, and that time I'll make something proper,
so I hope you won't abandon me. I'll do my best.

Please feel free to let me know your impressions or complaints via email, messenger,
mixi, Twitter, or similar services.
