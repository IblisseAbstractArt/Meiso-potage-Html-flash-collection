プロダクトゲーム Information Game

『Inaba Tewi’s Acrobatic Mochi-Pounding Rush』

Nice to meet those of you who are new here, and thank you as always to everyone else.
I’m Mi~ya, in charge of gluttony at Meisou Potage.

This time, I had planned to make something with a different concept for Winter Comiket,
but in the end, just as if to say “the handmade CD for when you’re in a bind,”
I made this under the title “Mochi Flavor.”
(It has finally stopped even being a drink...)

Since the CD title is “Mochi Flavor,”
I wondered if I could make a game featuring Tewi and mochi as a motif,
and after thinking it over, it became a game in this form.

Handling rigid bodies is difficult… or rather, troublesome... lol
This time, it undeniably feels even more half-assed than usual, but



As I write this, I’m incredibly sleepy after staying up all night…

Earlier, when I was writing the “How to Play” instructions,
I was half-asleep and wrote something that made absolutely no sense...


---------------------------
Simply move your mouse or pen tablet swiftly over the mochi,
and Tewi will pound the mochi in that direction.

Keep hitting it consecutively as much as possible,
and while taking care
“not to let the mochi stretch too far sideways”
and
“not to eat the mochi during a lecture,”
please make it.
---------------------------


...A lecture? What on earth was I talking about... It’s so chaotic that I don’t understand it anymore…
Anyway, since I’ve gone beyond my limits,
this may be brief, but that concludes my afterword.


To everyone who picked up this CD,
to Rengoku Teien, whose music we borrowed,
and to the original creator, the shrine maiden himself, ZUN,
thank you very much.

Good night... zzzzzzz


BGM used:
