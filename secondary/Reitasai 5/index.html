Notification regarding a setting error in the typing game "Touhou Kotodama Matsuri"

We apologize for this announcement coming so close to the release date, but there are a few errors in the settings of the game "Touhou Kotodama Matsuri" included in the final product.

  • After defeating Tewi, you can follow up with a bomb by pressing the Enter key.
  • In Tewi's last spell, you cannot erase "South 2nd Round 5th Honba" unless you type "nan ikkyoku gohonba".

Fortunately, there are no major problems in terms of game progression.
However, if you don't want to bully Tewi unnecessarily or waste bombs, please refrain from pressing the Enter key at the relevant point.
