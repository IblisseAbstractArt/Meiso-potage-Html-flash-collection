Comic Market 80 Participation Information

8/13 (Sat) Ne-28a

We will be distributing a newly published Touhou fan-made Flash animation collection, a hand-burned CD titled "Meisou Potage Cola Flavor".

Additionally, we will be distributing commissioned works from Sleipnir-sama, including "Yuyu-sama ~hungry for life~ with PV" and "The City Death, the Wolf, and the World Tree ~Prologue~".

In addition, we plan to bring in some older CDs by Meisou Potage. Thank you for your support.

作品紹介 Introduction

"Hey (´・ω・`) Sorry, it's me again."

Now a regular feature, this is the fifth installment in the "○○ Flavor" series of hand-burned CDs by the now-famous, meandering potage artist.
